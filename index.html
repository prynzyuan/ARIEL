To the girl who slowly became someone special

I don't know exactly when it happened, but at first, I kept telling myself not to develop feelings for you.

pero, little by little, what I thought was just a small crush became something more. Before I even realized it, you had become someone I genuinely liked.

there's also something I've been wanting to tell you. I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even just for a moment, because you'd understand how amazing you really are. hindi ko alam paano yon, but somehow, you always find your way into my thoughts. I just can't seem to stop thinking about you

hindi ko alam kung saan tayo dadalhin ng feelings ko, pero gusto pa kita makilala pa, and make more memories with you, and see where things could go between us. That's why I want to ask you something from the bottom of my heart.

I even started learning how to solve a Rubik's Cube, just so I'd have something to teach you and another reason to talk to you. it might seem like a small thing, but I guess that's one of the ways you've inspired me without even knowing it.

Honestly, I really enjoy talking to you. Diko talaga ma explain kung paano, pero may something sayo na gustong gusto ko, tuwing mag kausap tayo lalo kong na fefeel na sure ako sayo.

So, I want to ask you something I've been wanting to say for a while.

Would you let me court you? ♡

You don't have to rush your answer. I just wanted to be honest with you and let you know that, somewhere along the way, you became more than just a crush to me.
